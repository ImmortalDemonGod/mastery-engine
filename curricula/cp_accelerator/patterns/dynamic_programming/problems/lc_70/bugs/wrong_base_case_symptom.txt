# Bug Symptom: Wrong Base Case in Climbing Stairs

## What You'll Observe

Your solution returns wrong answers for n=2 and propagates errors to all larger n.

## Expected Behavior

- `climbStairs(2)` → `2` (two ways: 1+1 or 2)
- `climbStairs(3)` → `3`
- `climbStairs(5)` → `8`

## Actual Behavior

- `climbStairs(2)` → `1` ❌ (misses the "take 2 steps" option)
- `climbStairs(3)` → `2` ❌
- `climbStairs(5)` → `5` ❌

Every answer is off because the base case for n=2 is wrong.

## Debugging Guide

1. **Manually count ways for n=2**: How many distinct paths are there?
2. **Check your base case**: Does it handle n=2 correctly?
3. **Key insight**: For n=2, there are TWO ways (1+1 and 2), not one

## Conceptual Understanding

DP base cases are the foundation of the entire computation. A wrong base case
propagates errors to ALL larger values. Always verify your base cases by hand
before trusting the recurrence for larger inputs.
